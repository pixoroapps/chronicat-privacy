Privacy Policy

Chronicat: Cat Diary & AI · Last updated: 22 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixoro Apps ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Chronicat: Cat Diary & AI mobile application (the "App"). By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who We Are

The App is developed and operated by Pixoro Apps, located in Ukraine.

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: pixoro.apps@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • Chronicat is a diary and AI assistant that helps you track your cat's life (feeding, weight, medications, vet visits, notes, and other events).
  • The data you create about your cat and your AI chat history are stored locally on your device by default.
  • When you use the AI assistant, the content of your questions and relevant cat data are sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a response.
  • Subscription purchases are processed by the app store (Google Play), not by us. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • In the future, we plan to offer optional cloud synchronization of your cat events via a third-party backend (Supabase). This Policy will be updated before that feature is enabled, and sync will be optional.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information you provide (stored locally on your device)

  • Cat profile data: name, photo, breed, date of birth, sex, neuter/spay status, microchip number, allergies, chronic conditions, and similar details you choose to enter.
  • Event and diary data: feeding, water, play/activity, weight, vet visits, medications, vaccinations, parasite treatments, litter/toileting, symptoms, grooming, behavioral notes, photos, and free-text notes you create.
  • AI chat history: the questions you ask the AI assistant and the responses you receive.

By default, all of the above is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to or accessible by us, except as described in Section 3.3 (AI processing) and Section 5 (future cloud sync).

3.2 Subscription and purchase information

When you purchase a subscription or start a free trial, the transaction is handled by the applicable app store (Google Play). We may receive limited, non-sensitive information from the store to confirm your subscription status (for example, a purchase token or subscription state). We do not collect or store your full payment card number or billing address — these are handled entirely by the app store.

3.3 Information processed by the AI assistant

When you use the AI assistant, the content of your message and relevant context (such as selected cat profile details and recent events) is transmitted to a third-party AI provider, OpenAI, L.L.C. ("OpenAI"), solely to generate a response. This processing is necessary to provide the AI feature. Please review the AI provider's own privacy terms for how they handle data submitted through their API.

3.4 Technical and usage information

We (or our service providers) may automatically collect limited technical data to operate and improve the App, such as:

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • Crash reports and diagnostic logs.
  • Anonymous or aggregated usage analytics (for example, which features are used).

This data does not identify you personally and is used to maintain stability and improve the product.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide the core diary and tracking features of the App.
  • Provide AI assistant responses based on the data you choose to share with it.
  • Process and validate subscriptions and free trials (via the app store).
  • Send reminders and notifications you enable (for example, medication or vaccination reminders).
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance and features.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your cat's health data for advertising.

5. Cloud Synchronization (Planned Feature)

The App currently stores your data locally. In a future version, we plan to offer optional cloud synchronization of your cat events using a third-party backend provider, Supabase (operated by Supabase, Inc.). When and if this feature becomes available:

  • Sync will be opt-in — your data will only leave your device if you choose to enable it (for example, by creating an account).
  • Synced data will be transmitted and stored on Supabase infrastructure to allow backup and access across your devices.
  • We will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, request your consent before enabling this feature.

Until you enable cloud sync, your event and diary data remain on your device.

6. How We Share Information

We share information only as described here:

  • App stores (Google Play): to process subscriptions, trials, and renewals.
  • AI provider (OpenAI): to generate assistant responses, as described in Section 3.3.
  • Analytics and crash-reporting providers: to maintain and improve the App.
  • Cloud backend (Supabase): only if and when you enable optional cloud sync (Section 5).
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users or others.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App will typically remove locally stored data; deleted data may not be recoverable.
  • Subscription records are retained by the app store according to its policies.
  • AI chat content submitted to the AI provider is handled according to that provider's retention terms.
  • If you enable cloud sync in the future, synced data will be retained until you delete it or close your account.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. Because your primary data is stored locally, its security also depends on the security of your device (for example, device lock, encryption, and OS updates). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Because most data is stored locally, you can exercise many of these rights directly within the App (by editing or deleting entries) or by uninstalling the App. For other requests, contact us at pixoro.apps@gmail.com.

For California users (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information as defined by California law. You have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
