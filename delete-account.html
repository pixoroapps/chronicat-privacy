Delete Your Account

Chronicat: Cat Diary & AI — developed by Pixoro Apps

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Chronicat and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Manage account
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account:

Email: pixoro.apps@gmail.com

Include the subject line "Delete my Chronicat account". We process requests within 30 days.

Delete only some of your data (keep your account)

To remove your cats, timeline events, and AI chat history while staying signed in:

  1. Open Chronicat and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Manage account
  3. Tap Erase local data and confirm

This data lives only on your device, so it's erased instantly — no request or waiting period needed. Your account and subscription stay active.
